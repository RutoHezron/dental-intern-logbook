# User guide (interns)

## First time

Open the app and choose one of:

- **Open the demo** to explore a fictional 12-month internship (Dr Alex Mwangi). Nothing you do there affects your own logbook. To leave it, go to **Settings → Start my own logbook**.
- **Set up my internship**: enter your name, training centre, start date and rotation order. You can change dates, extensions and supervisors later under **Rotations**.

**Your logbook is stored on your phone.** On first launch the app sets up its storage on the phone, and the welcome screen confirms it. No account or signal is needed.

- **Android app:** your data stays in the app's own storage. A backup copy is saved automatically every day to `Documents/DentalLogbook` (the last 7 days are kept). It survives even if the app is uninstalled.
- **Browser:** use **Add to Home screen** so the browser keeps your data permanently. Then save a backup to your phone's files or cloud drive weekly (**Settings → Phone storage → Save backup to phone**); the app reminds you.

## Logging a patient (about 30 seconds)

1. Tap **Log**, or tap a favourite procedure on **Today**.
2. **Patient no.**: the hospital or outpatient number only. Never a name or phone number. Recent patients appear as one-tap buttons. For a new patient, add the birth year (needed for the under-17 paediatric rule) and sex.
3. **Procedure**: tap a favourite, or search **All procedures**.
4. **Teeth**: tap them on the chart. Use **Primary** for deciduous teeth.
5. **Your level**, which is never pre-filled:

   | Level | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | 1 | Observed |
   | 2 | Assisted |
   | 3 | Whole procedure, supervisor present throughout |
   | 4 | Whole procedure, supervisor available |
   | 5 | Independent |

6. **Supervisor**: the last one you used is chosen for you.
7. Check **Will be logged**. It shows the KMPDC code each item counts toward and the rule used, or why it does not count (for example, an extraction during periodontology).
8. **Save**, or **Save & next** to keep the procedure and supervisor for the next patient.

Optional details live under *Diagnosis, investigations, referral and notes*. OMF 1 counts only when you record a diagnosis; the app never guesses one.

**Activities without a patient** (postmortems, diagnostic sessions, ward rounds, casualty calls, case presentations, CPD, community activities, case follow-up steps, weekly reports): tap **Log** → **Activity (no patient)**.

### Warnings you may see

| Warning | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Late entry | Logged more than 3 days after the procedure. Your supervisor will see this. |
| Possible duplicate | Same patient, procedure and tooth on the same day. |
| Unusual for this rotation | Recorded as exposure, but it does not count toward a code. |
| Looks like a name / phone / ID | Remove identifiers from free text. |

## Your logbook

- **Logbook → Records**: search by patient no., procedure, tooth, diagnosis, code or supervisor, and filter by rotation, status, level or dates. Tap a record to edit it.
- **Logbook → By code**: progress for every KMPDC code — verified out of the minimum, plus entries awaiting verification (hatched), the highest verified level, and status (met, on track, at risk, behind, not met).
- Code chips: **solid** = verified, **dashed** = awaiting verification, **red outline** = returned by your supervisor (fix and save to resubmit).
- Verified entries cannot be edited or deleted. This protects your evidence.

## Getting entries signed

1. Open **Supervisor sign-off** (from Logbook, More, or the reminder on Today).
2. Tap **Hand the phone to Dr …** and give your supervisor the phone.
3. Your supervisor reviews each entry. Any notes beside an entry are prompts. For each one they choose **Verify**, **Different level** (the level they observed) or **Return** (with what to fix). **Verify all remaining as claimed** speeds this up.
4. They tap **Sign** and enter their own PIN. The first time, they choose it.
5. They hand the phone back. Verified entries are now locked. Returned entries show a red outline; fix the visit and save it to send it back.

The supervisor's PIN is stored only as a protected code on your phone. Never ask for it or record it.

## Case reports

The logbook expects ten case reports per rotation. **Case reports** shows how many are verified in each rotation.

1. Open a visit (or a patient's case timeline) and tap **Write a case report**. The patient's visits are linked, and the facts are drafted from what you recorded: age and sex, complaint, findings, investigations, diagnosis, and each procedure with its date and your level. The hospital number is **not** copied into the report.
2. Check the draft, then write the **discussion and learning points** in your own words. **Draft from linked visits** fills only empty sections and never overwrites what you wrote.
3. Choose the supervisor and tap **Send for verification**.
4. At your next **Supervisor sign-off**, the report appears for them to read and verify or return. Once verified it is locked, and it fills a row of the printed *Log of Case Reports*.

## Printing your logbook pages

**Print logbook** (from the Logbook or More menu) produces the KMPDC case and procedure log for a rotation in the book's layout. It is filled from your verified entries and ready for your supervisor's signature and stamp. Each row shows a short digital reference that links it to the signed record in the app. Entries beyond the book's rows go on continuation sheets. You can include entries still awaiting verification if your supervisor prefers to sign the paper directly.

## Assessment forms

**Assessment forms** lists each rotation's monthly, mid-rotation, end-of-rotation and overall forms, with due dates and status. Open a form at the meeting:

- Your name and the rotation dates are already filled in.
- The supervisor's *progress in achieving logbook targets* can be drafted from your logbook with **Fill from logbook**, then edited.
- In the end-of-rotation evaluation, a grade of 0 or 1 cannot be saved without reasons, as the logbook requires.
- A snapshot of your logbook counts is attached as evidence.
- Use **Print view → Print** for signatures, names, registration numbers and stamps.

## Finishing your internship

Near the end of your last rotation, **Assessment forms** shows an **End of internship** panel at the top:

- **Ready for KMPDC?** checks what is still outstanding: rotations not finished, forms not complete, rotations not yet recorded as completed, logbook minimums not met, and entries still waiting for verification.
- **5.8 End of internship evaluation** holds the coordinator's recommendation and the Medical Superintendent's decision (recommended for registration, extension, or irredeemable).
- **5.9 Verification of completion** fills in your rotation dates from the calendar, including extensions. Add the coordinator's remark for each rotation, then print it for signature and the hospital stamp.

## Diary

Write a few lines after a memorable case, using the prompts: what was interesting, what you learned, what challenged you, what you would do differently, what to revise, and what you need more exposure to. Tag entries ⭐ important, 📚 learning, ⚠️ challenging, 🧠 revision or 🏆 milestone. Diary entries feed your reports and portfolio, and stay private unless you tick *Share with my supervisor*.

## Reports

**Reports** offers weekly, monthly, rotation, whole-internship and custom reports. Each shows:

- **This period**, **Previous** and **Cumulative**, side by side;
- procedures by level and area, diagnoses, and progress per code (*+n this period*);
- areas requiring further exposure, learning themes, and supervisor feedback;
- insights, each labelled *recorded data* or *interpretation*, with the numbers behind it.

Use **Print / PDF** to save or print, and the CSV buttons for spreadsheets. **Finalise report** seals a copy before you share it. If your records change afterwards, the report tells you.

## Portfolio

**Portfolio** assembles your internship from your records: overview, totals, a month-by-month timeline, procedure portfolio, rotation performance, competency development, strengths, areas to develop, and highlights from your diary. The **draft reflection** is built only from your data, and each sentence shows what it is based on. Edit it into your own words; nothing is submitted automatically.

## Sync and backup

- The status at the top shows **Saved on this device**, **Synced**, **Pending sync (n)**, **Offline** or **Sync error**.
- If your build has sync enabled: **Settings → Account & sync**, enter your email, then type the 6-digit code you receive. Your logbook backs up and appears on any device where you sign in.
- Either way: **Settings → Export full backup** saves a file of everything on the device. Keep it somewhere safe.

## Things to know

- Counting rules are **defaults** until your training centre confirms them (Settings → Counting rules).
- Counts show **recorded exposure**. They do not certify competence; your supervisors assess that.
- Targets should never decide a patient's treatment.
